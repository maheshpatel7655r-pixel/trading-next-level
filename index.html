🟢 WHEN TO BUY CE (Call) Bullish

1. Breakout Entry: When a 5-min candle breaks & CLOSES above 5-Min Candle High. Buy nearest ATM Call.

2. Support Bounce: If market drops to Major Support (S1) and forms a green hammer -> Buy CE for bounce.

🎯 Target: Major Resistance (R1) 🛑 Stoploss: 5-Min Candle High
🔴 WHEN TO BUY PE (Put) Bearish

1. Breakdown Entry: When a 5-min candle breaks & CLOSES below 5-Min Candle Low. Buy nearest ATM Put.

2. Reversal at R1: If market rallies to Major Resistance (R1) and makes a rejection red candle -> Buy PE.

🎯 Target: Major Support (S1) 🛑 Stoploss: 5-Min Candle Low
🛡️ WHEN TO SELL OPTIONS Sideways

Range-Bound Days: If price stays between 5-Min High & Low, market is sideways. Do NOT buy options!

Strangle Income: Sell OTM Call ABOVE Major Resistance (R1) + Sell OTM Put BELOW Major Support (S1) for 100% theta decay.
